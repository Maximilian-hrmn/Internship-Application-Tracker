I made my coding environment "stupid" by disabling all major AI-suggestion mechanisms in order to force me to profoundly learn
the language rather than letting the AI do the work without me actually understanding anything. What I didn't turn off were the commit message
suggestions because I felt that it wouldn't affect my learning progress and would improve the readability of the whole project. Furthermore, I
used AI to guide me through the whole project. I specifically instructed the AI to only guide me, without giving me the answers. The AI gave me
suggestions on how to proceed and gave me the functions I needed to reach my goal. In order to prevent it from giving me the answer, I made it give
me the functions based on another topic.
